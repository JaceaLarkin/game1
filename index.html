How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Every move gives monsters a turn.

Defeat monsters for XP and gold. Level up to earn skill points, then spend them in your skill tree. Visit the shop to buy upgrades.

    Progression

    Grow stronger

    Each monster grants XP and gold. Level-ups increase maximum health, restore a little health, and award one skill point. XP needed rises as your level grows.

    Choose a path

    Build your hero

    Warrior skills boost attack, Survivor skills increase health, and Scavenger skills improve treasure and monster rewards. Learn a node to unlock the connected skill below it.

    Spend wisely

    Visit the shop

    Healing potions restore health. Sharpened blades raise attack, while reinforced armor reduces incoming damage. Upgrades get more expensive as you improve them.

    Find the stairs. See how deep you can go.

    Character progression

    Skill tree

    Choose from 15 connected upgrades across Warrior, Survivor, and Scavenger paths. Progress through Foundation, Growth, Specialty, Mastery, and Capstone sections.
